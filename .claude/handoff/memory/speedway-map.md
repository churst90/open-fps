Since 2026-09-14 (session 3) `OpenFPS.Server/maps/speedway.json` is the map a player lands on — it sets `IsDefault: true`, which `MapManager.DefaultMapId` reads and `Program.HandleLogin` puts on the session. Since 2026-09-14 it is ST LOUIS: a 1.25-mile EGG (turns 1-2 R137, turns 3-4 R183, 10 deg banking), 29 `nascar_v8` plus one `police_v8` pace car that cannot keep up, and you SPAWN IN THE INFIELD so cars pass on every side. Four `pa_speaker` horns at the centre say "Welcome to the Saint Louis raceway" every 20 s (espeak, rendered by `tools/gen_announcements.py`). The walls are now derived FROM the centreline (offset along the local normal), so any track shape works, a concrete wall the whole way round, a grandstand deck you spawn on, and NO ambience bed. The map is GENERATED by `tools/gen_speedway.py` — edit the script and re-run it, never the JSON.

Cars lap via `Track` + `RaceLine` (OpenFPS.Common); speeds come from track curvature and grip, not from a script. Only the nearest `ClientAudioSystem.EngineVoiceBudget` engines are SYNTHESIZED (the rest borrow one of those), because one V8 costs ~12% of a core in release and ~30% in debug — hence `run-gtk-client.sh` now builds Release by default. The budget follows measured mixer load, and is held for 3 s after a load or spawn because the reading cannot be trusted then — see [[engine-audio-next-step]].

**Why:** the owner asked for a NASCAR track you hear on login, with wall reflections.
**How to apply:** audition with `--speedway` (no server needed) and debug reflections with `--speedway speedway probe`, which prints what bounces off what and when. See [[engine-synthesis-state]] and [[build-on-tmpfs]].

**Two traps a full 30-car field springs (both fixed 2026-09-14):**
- `setSoftwareChannels` is a PRE-`init` FMOD call; after init it returns ERR_INITIALIZED and the
  limit silently stays at 64 real voices. 30 cars = 30 engines + up to 60 reflections, so FMOD
  virtualises the quietest ~25 and the set flips as cars approach and recede — on a SYNTHESIZED
  voice that is heard as extreme pitch jumpiness on every pass-by, arriving a minute or two in.
  Watch the `real/playing` column on the Mixer load line.
- LiteNetLib does NOT fragment `DeliveryMethod.Unreliable`: past 1023 bytes it throws and the whole
  tick's world state is dropped. 8 cars fitted, 30 do not. `NetworkService.SendStateUpdate` now
  halves until each chunk fits and `ClientWorldState.SyncState` merges same-tick chunks by entity id.
  Heard as some cars freezing for about a second mid-lap.
- `ClientWorldState.UpdateInterpolation`'s playback clock was set once and then advanced by the
  CLIENT's own dt for ever — an independent clock with no correction. When it drifted outside the
  snapshot buffer (10 snapshots = 333 ms at the 30 Hz tick, playback 100 ms back = 233 ms of margin)
  NO bracketing pair existed and every entity in the world silently held position. Now rate-corrected
  toward `newest - InterpolationDelay`, snapped past 0.5 s out, and 30 snapshots of history. Heard as
  some cars stopping for a second mid-lap — "some" because only a near car's bearing changes enough
  to notice. Test: `AClientClockThatRunsFastDoesNotFreezeTheWorld`.
- **Doppler is computed from a position the game thread only refreshes every ~22 ms**, while the
  attribute loop runs at 250 Hz — so the pitch was a 45 Hz staircase written 250 times a second. The
  step goes as v^2/d: 0.86% at 50 m (a glide) but 8% — 1.34 semitones — at 5 m, heard as an
  "auto-tune" on close passes only. `FmodAudioProvider.DeadReckon` carries the source forward on its
  own velocity (capped 50 ms) for both Doppler and panning. Test: `AClosePassGlidesInPitchInsteadOfStepping`.
- Engine reflections are capped to the nearest `EchoCarLimit` (6) cars. All 30 getting 2 each meant
  60 reflection voices against 30 direct, nearly all off the SAME grandstand wall behind the
  listener — heard as "I'm hearing only the reflections". One wall returns one room response however
  many cars are in front of it.
- `SoundEmitterComponent.RepeatIntervalSeconds` replays any emitter every N seconds with SILENCE
  between — a PA, a foghorn, a station bell. Not LoopOne: the gap is what makes it a landmark.
- Turn keys: J/L were reversed (a positive `LookDelta.X` DECREASES yaw, which turns left; J emitted
  negative). Now a tap is 45 deg, shift+tap 1 deg, hold sweeps after 0.35 s — for J, L, K and O
  alike. Test: `TurnKeyTests`.

**Map defect found 2026-09-14 (session 6), not yet fixed:** the generator sets the grandstand back
from the front straight's NEAREST point, but the straight is a tangent between unequal circles and
slants 46 m, so the track runs THROUGH the grandstand deck for ~120 m before turn 4 and the outer
lane is 4.5 m from the deck at start/finish. And `--speedway` has no acoustic worker, so it can
never reproduce an occlusion fault. See [[audio-ghosts-and-stutters]].
