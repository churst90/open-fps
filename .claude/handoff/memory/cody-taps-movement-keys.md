Cody moves by **tapping** the movement keys as well as holding them, and expects one footstep per
press. One tap is one fixed tick at 4.5 m/s = **15 cm of ground**, which is why a half-metre stride
rule made him press three or four times for one sound ("every 2 or 3 presses").

He also holds them, and judges the two separately: *"holding down w should be a consistent walking
pace, holding shift down should be a consistent running pace like a shotgun of footsteps."*

**Why:** any rule keyed to distance walked has to be checked against a 15 cm tap AND against a held
key, and the two give different faults. See [[gait-from-the-body]].
**How to apply:** test movement audio both ways. [[ear-test-harness]], [[synthesis-by-listening]].
