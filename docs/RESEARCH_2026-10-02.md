## Doors and open sides of buildings

### What counts as a room today
- A map room (`acoustic_region`) is measured from the walls around it when the map loads. A face counts
  as wall when at least 35 % of it is covered (`CompositeAcoustics.cs:44`).
- A region is indoors when 4 or more of its 6 faces are walls (`MapManager.cs:478`,
  `MinimumCoveredFaces = 4`). One or two walls are never a room. A roof is not needed: four walls
  and no roof (a yard) is a room.
- Player-built buildings (composites) use the same 4-face rule, but every face is given a material,
  open ones too (`CompositeAcoustics.cs:133`, `:330`). So a composite's open side counts as a wall.

### Doors against open sides
- A door is an opening whose size follows how far the door is open (`DoorSystem.cs:87-91`). The door
  leaf is real geometry in the Steam Audio scene.
- An open side of a map region becomes an opening (`AcousticVolumeGenerator.cs:150-202`), but only
  one opening is allowed per pair of rooms (`:196`). If a building has a door to the outdoors, its
  open side to the outdoors is dropped. A gap smaller than about 65 % of a face counts as wall.
- Composites link only parts that are both a Portal and a Door (`CompositeService.cs:631-642`).
  With the face fault above, a composite's only openings are its doors.

**Suggestion:** every side that is not closed in is an opening; a door is an opening with a leaf.
Make uncovered composite faces open; allow one opening per open face, not one per pair of rooms.
Keep "a room is 4 or more walled faces, roof optional".

### Why the city does not wash into the lobby
1. **Turned down twice (likely the main cause).** A street sound feeds the outdoor reverb after its
   own route filtering and occlusion (`FmodAudioProvider.cs:2778-2779`). The outdoor reverb is then
   turned down again by the doorway leak.
2. **The doorway leak is the old one-room law.** It uses the nearest opening that joins the
   listener's room straight to the outside (`FmodAudioProvider.cs:3998-4013`) and ignores the new
   routes through openings. Two openings away, the outdoor reverb gets nothing.
3. **Wrong reverb.** Indoors, the outdoor reverb uses the listener's own trace (`:1836-1845`). What
   leaks in is street sound with the lobby's reverb, not the street's wash.
4. **Little to leak.** There are no ambience beds by design, and only voices that are playing feed
   the outdoor reverb. That matches "only when cars pass the open door".

**Suggestion, in order:**
1. Send to the reverb from before route filtering and occlusion. Small.
2. Weight each room's reverb by the routes through openings, placed at the last opening.
   About 100-150 lines.
3. Trace the outdoor reverb heard from inside from a point about 2 m outside the nearest open
   outside opening.

## Beacons
- **Level:** 66 dB at 1 m (`BeaconAids.cs:52`), about 1 dB under a footstep. Raising this number
  gains only 45 % of the change, because the loudness law compresses it.
- **Range:** door 12 m, item 10 m, vehicle 25 m, the nearest 3 (`:41-43`). A blip every 1.6 s.
  The level cuts off sharply at the edge of the range.
- **Reverb:** beacons send to the reverb, so in a live lobby with three doors they sound all around.
- **Settings:** there is no beacon volume setting, and nothing in the code accounts for loud
  surroundings.

**Suggestion:**
- A rendered gain, +4 dB to start, saved in beacons.json and set with `/beacons louder|quieter`.
- Door range about 6 m with a 2 m fade at the edge.
- Little or no reverb send.
- Lift beacons by up to 6 dB when a louder sound is near.

## Disconnect, Escape, loading
- **Disconnect:** both clients say "Disconnected" and then stay in the game window with voices
  running. Nothing is torn down, and there is no way back to the menu.
  - Reconnect can re-login with the credentials kept in memory.
  - The server always spawns a login at the default map's spawn, so position is lost.
- **Escape:** GTK asks "Quit OpenFPS?" and quits without telling the server. Windows sends a logout
  that the server has no handler for.
- **Loading:** a VPS login took 8 s on the last log (city, 6,408 entity definitions, one reliable
  message each, so it is bound by round trips). The friend is farther away.
  - Acoustics and the Steam Audio scene take under 1.5 s.
  - Progress percentages already exist (`UpdateLoadingStatus`); GTK does not speak them.
- **Create account on GTK:** the Windows client has it (`MenuWindow.cs:104-112`,
  `ClientRunner.cs:140-181`). Porting it is about 50 lines with no protocol change.

## Weapons and the inventory
- There is no bag. Items stay world entities. They are in your hands (right and left; a two-handed
  item takes both) or stowed "on your back" (`InventoryComponent`, limited to 25 kg).
- Weapons fire only from your hands. Draw has no key (only `/draw`, `/equip`, `/unsling`).
- Nothing is saved between sessions.

## Car starter
- The starter is synthesised (`EngineSynth.StarterSound`, `EngineSynth.cs:1247-1294`) and summed
  into the engine block. T while riding turns the key.
- **Likely cause: level, not a missing trigger.** In the cab the block reaches you through the panels
  at about -30 dB, plus a seal leak. Outside it leaves through the bay at about -16.5 dB. The
  starter's 400-1200 Hz arrives 26-35 dB down and the idle covers it.
- The bench plays the block at full level and cranks for 0.9 s. The game cranks for 0.34-0.53 s. The
  starter was approved on a path 16-30 dB louder and twice as long.
- **Confirm in the client log:** "Engine voice started", then "Riding entity", then
  "[SAY] You turn the key."
- **Fix:** give the starter its own path into the cabin (about -15 to -20 dB) and outside, hold the
  key 0.6-0.8 s, and add level tests.

## The .357 sample and gunfire
- **The video has no labels.** It is one still photo for 43 s.
- **None of its shots can be a spec.** All six shots clip (650-760 samples at full scale) and carry
  about 1 s of reverb. Only the mechanical sounds are usable: cylinder clicks, the ejector thunk,
  and brass cases ringing at 2,875, 6,160 and 9,990 Hz.
- **A clean .357 spec is already here:** the NIJ/Cadre Ruger .357 set
  (`inbox/weapons/cadreforensics/Ruger357_Zoom.zip`), unclipped at nearly every position.
- **Measured against the NIJ Glock at 90°:**
  - The .357 decays more slowly (1.4-1.5 ms to -10 dB against 1.0 ms).
  - It is 3-8 dB stronger at 2-4 kHz side-on, which fits a cylinder-gap blast.
  - Its positive phase is no longer than the Glock's.
- **A demo to listen to:** `inbox/gunfire-357-2026-10-02/demo/` holds real .357, synth .357, synth
  .357 with a cylinder-gap blast, real Glock, and the game's Glock today. The synth is a Python copy
  of `WeaponSynth`.
- **Faults found in today's gunfire:**
  - The lab's fitted values never reached the game: Glock positive phase 0.30 ms against 0.42 ms in
    game, and high-pass 250 Hz against 80 Hz.
  - The game's Glock is 6-12 dB too strong at 125-250 Hz and peaks at 2 kHz where real pistols peak
    at 1 kHz. One pole on pink noise cannot make that peak.
  - An unknown cartridge falls back to 159 dB without a warning.
- **A .357 preset needs:**
  - about 410 m/s from a 6-inch barrel, which is just supersonic;
  - about 164 dB;
  - no bolt or ejection sound;
  - a cylinder reload;
  - a cylinder-gap layer about 0.3-0.5 ms ahead of the muzzle blast;
  - blast directivity, which is not modelled today.

## Weather
- **Modelled:**
  - clock, season, temperature, humidity;
  - wind as a vector plus gustiness;
  - precipitation as a number from 0 to 1;
  - four fronts lerped between (`WorldEnvironmentSystem.cs`).
- **Pressure** is fixed per map from its altitude. A 30 mb weather front changes air absorption by
  only a few per cent, so modelling it is not worth it.
- **What the audio uses:**
  - Temperature, humidity and pressure feed ISO 9613-1 air absorption.
  - Temperature sets the speed of sound for Doppler, but echoes use a fixed 343 m/s. That is two
    speeds of sound.
- **Silent:** rain only changes footstep materials. Wind is stored but never played.
- **Bug:** wind adds 0.1 × the wind speed to the listener's velocity (`ClientAudioSystem.cs:489,497`).
  Uniform wind does not shift pitch, so gusts bend the pitch of every sound.
- **A fuller model would add:**
  - rain rate in mm/h, used for the rain sound;
  - surface wetness that lasts after the rain, used for tyres and footsteps;
  - wind and temperature against height, used for refraction beyond 150 m;
  - turbulence, used for wind at the ear;
  - snow cover.
