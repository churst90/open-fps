# Listening spots on the city map

Places to check rooms, reflections and the outdoors quickly. Type `/tp` and the three numbers: east,
north, height (the same order C reads out). Heights are just above the floor; you land on it.

## Indoors

| Where | /tp | Listen for |
|---|---|---|
| Airport terminal, middle of the concourse | `/tp 216 58 0.5` | A big hall with an acoustic ceiling: a long room, but not a cathedral |
| Airport terminal, by the glass doors to the apron | `/tp 223 30 0.5` | The same hall, one side of glass |
| Hangar | `/tp 254 190 0.5` | A steel box the size of a church |
| Parking garage, level 0, middle | `/tp -66 62 0.5` | Low concrete ceiling, open sides |
| Parking garage, level 0, by the open edge | `/tp -118 62 0.5` | The street coming in over the parapet |
| Union Building, ground floor corridor | `/tp 20 67 0.5` | A long narrow corridor |
| Union Building, flat 00B | `/tp 26 28 0.5` | A furnished flat |
| Union Building, stairwell | `/tp 14 28 0.5` | A hard stairwell |
| Marlow Tower, flat 01F | `/tp -14 -85 0.5` | Another flat, the other side of Main Street |
| 64 Alder Street, living room | `/tp -215 130.5 0.5` | A small furnished house; front door on the street side |
| Calder Road station platform | `/tp 170 60 1.5` | A platform shelter |
| Tunnel, middle | `/tp 0 -250 0.5` | A road tunnel (traffic comes through) |

## Outdoors

| Where | /tp | Listen for |
|---|---|---|
| Main Street between Marlow Tower and Kestrel House | `/tp 8 -67 0.5` | A street canyon: facades both sides |
| Market Square | `/tp -66 190 0.5` | An open square |
| Calder Avenue pavement | `/tp 122 50 0.5` | A wide avenue with lawns, little to reflect |
| Alder Street, on the pavement | `/tp -296 124.5 0.5` | A quiet residential street |
| 49 Alder Street, back garden | `/tp -413 98 0.5` | A garden behind a house, hedges either side |
| The level crossing | `/tp 0 -180 0.5` | Open, the railway and the road |
| The apron | `/tp 258 59 0.5` | Wide open tarmac beside the terminal |

A clap (T) and a knock (Shift+E, beside a door) are the quickest way to hear a place.
