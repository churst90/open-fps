code"). New tests for ClientAudioSystem's voice selection, VehicleShadow.Apply, EngineReflections,
the acoustic worker without Steam Audio, and every DSP callback. One bug found and fixed (below).

    DOTNET_CLI_USE_MSBUILD_SERVER=0 ~/.dotnet/dotnet test OpenFPS.Tests --artifacts-path <dir> \
        -nodeReuse:false -p:UseSharedCompilation=false --no-build \

run the rain placement test also timed out under the collector (the rain survey is slow when

classes alone on the same code. Line counts are per source line, merged over every class in the

The figures are for the slice, so they are lower than the whole suite would give. The 09-24 figures

## The new tests

### ClientAudioSelectionTests (26)

Through ClientAudioHarness (real facade, voice budget and acoustic worker; the test's clock). The
harness's mixer load can now be set, to drive the budget's control loop.

- Engines: built two an update, nearest first; the nearest car of every preset gets a live engine on
  top of the budget; at most twelve cars borrow, the nearest twelve of the rest, and past that a car
  has no voice; a borrowed voice reads a live car of its own preset at its own ring offset, with its
  own velocity, in the global region; at most six front voices, the six nearest, at the same level
  reference as the car's own voice.
- The car you sit in: one interior voice (riding with the head, no path, gain from its reference
  distance, your region), one voice per cabin path, no front voice; all let go when you step out.
- Every physical kind (machine, bell, rail, water, fire, foliage, flow, shore, aircraft) placed at its
  declared level and size from the same lookup the ranking uses, its level handed on for the ear
  model, and (where not spread over places) its gain times its own headroom's return.
- Machines: ranked by rendered gain, not distance (a 65 dB condenser at 12 m beats the furthest of ten
  59 dB window units); the ranking reads the ear model's correction; keep bias and hold, then the set
  follows the listener.
- The budget under load: front voices go first, then machines, then borrowed voices, then cars (never
  below two, one machine, four borrowed); when the load falls the cars come back first. Nothing is
  given up during the 3 s after a scene load, or for spikes shorter than 0.75 s.
- Woods: from 250 m a wood of 25 trees is one voice standing for 25 trees, no tree voiced; inside it,
  the wood holds one machine slot, its nearest trees the other nine, and it stands for the rest.
- Rain: far patches on their own sides, within the survey's rings, carrying their paths; near drops
  on the ground within 2.5 m or on the listener, placed by the drop law.
- A repeating emitter: first firing staggered by its id, then once an interval, never over itself.
- A beacon whose category the map forbids is stopped (through the map policy, not the player's
  switches, which are written to ~/.config).
- A siren placed at its declared level, and stopped when its car leaves the world.

### VehicleShadowApplyTests (5)

VehicleShadow.Apply on a real ClientWorldState snapshot: a bus between darkens the bands and moves
with the bus; static walls are left to the scene; the loss is laid on top of the path's own; your own
ride and the source itself are never the barrier.

### EngineReflectionsTests (10)

Through the facade: an echo at the wall's image with the right delay and gain; not muffled by what
muffles the car, with its own air; a pillar in the mirrored path silences it; only standing solid
geometry reflects (SyncGeometry, with a rebuild); zero echoes per engine and a traced source start
nothing; a wall that stops answering fades where it was over 0.8 s and is let go; an echo that did not
start is asked for again; Forget stops a car's echoes and only its echoes; the budget goes to the
loudest reflections (audibility floor, EndFrame); a row of blocks is one wall and one voice.

### AsyncAcousticWorkerTests (6)

The real worker thread, bounded polling, no timing assertions: the latest request is answered by the
hand-rolled tracer and stamped with where it was asked; nothing simulated is claimed without Steam
Audio; Forget; a request with no world is dropped; when the tracer throws, a source keeps its last
answer and a new one gets a clear path; a worker never started disposes cleanly.

### DspCallbackTests (57)

Every DSP read callback run without FMOD: a stand-in for the DSP_STATE function table supplies the
user data and the channel clock, and the real private callback is called. Each one: finite output, no
allocation in the callback (GC.GetAllocatedBytesForCurrentThread after a warm-up), silence in gives
silence out where that holds, and levels where a law states them:

- MachineProcessor over 17 kinds of physical voice (window unit, mowers, airliner, siren, horns,
  crossing bell, rain, light rail, fountain and its tap, fire and its place, tree and its place,
  creek, shore): the ring at unity in both channels, sample for sample against a twin voice.
- EngineProcessor, TapProcessor, EchoProcessor: the voice at unity; a cabin tap plays exactly the
  block its engine's callback played at the same moment.
- SynthProcessor, GranularProcessor (a grain is a Hann window of its source at the 0.7 trim),
  EarWindProcessor (wind held), EarStage (its gain matches LoudnessCompensation.ShelfResponseDb within
  0.15 dB; slews at 6 dB/s), BoundaryProximityProcessor, MasterLimiter (equals a twin
  TruePeakLimiter), MasterDither (triangular, one step at most, RMS step/sqrt 6), MasterTap,
  OwnVoiceProcessor.
- SteamAudioDsp, AmbisonicBedDsp, TracedReverbDsp, TracedEchoDsp: their bail-out and non-finite
  paths, and the echo rig's capture at its input gain.
- Every callback with no state is silent or passes the mix through; a callback handed the wrong
  state returns a silent block and records the fault for the game thread.

## Bug found and fixed

**A preset's donor car churned the edge of the engine budget.** The nearest car of each preset is
given a live engine outside the budget, so cars of that preset can borrow from it. It was let go and
re-admitted each time its 2.5 s hold ran out; re-admitted, it was stamped as newly built, so it was
held again, ranked first and given a slot INSIDE the budget, which turned out the car at the budget's
edge. That engine was faded and rebuilt from nothing every 2.5 s, every borrowed voice restarted with
it, and the donor's echoes were forgotten, while nothing moved. It happens on any map with more cars
than the budget and a preset whose nearest car is outside it (the city: its two motorcycles, a bus).
Test: `ADonorOutsideTheBudgetDoesNotChurnTheCarsInIt` (cars 31 and 32 alternately stopped and
rebuilt, and five borrowed voices restarted, every cycle). Fix: ChooseLiveEngines finds the donors
before letting anything go and keeps them.

**Changes sound (for Cody):** the furthest live engines no longer drop out and rebuild every 2.5 s,
and distant borrowed cars no longer restart with them, on a map with more cars than the budget.
Unheard.

Not fixed: when a new donor is first admitted it is held for 2.5 s and takes a slot inside the budget
for that time, so one budget car is let go once and rebuilt 2.5 s later.

## Findings for Cody (nothing changed)

1. **The ear model's correction raises the rank of sounds the ear hears less of.** The machine
   ranking and VoiceManager.Audibility both multiply a voice's rank by the law's correction
   (EarTimbres.CorrectionDb). The correction is how much MORE gain the law gives a sound so it is
   heard at its real loudness, so it is positive for timbres the ear is deaf to: +20.8 dB for a 65 dB
   rumble at 25 Hz, about +9 to +10 dB for a 1 kHz tone or a 250 Hz band. A rumble therefore ranks as
   20 dB louder than an unmeasured sound of the same level, where the comments and EAR_MODEL.md say
   "the one that is heard more ranks higher". The ranking now matches the gain the mixer plays (so
   the two never disagree), but not loudness. Whether to rank on loudness instead is a design
   question; `TheMachineBudgetRanksOnTheGainTheLawPlaysItAt` pins the present behaviour.
2. **A shore voice is digital silence for about 11 s after it starts** (sea sand, a held 4.5 m/s
   wind: 524 blocks before the first non-zero sample). A shore that comes into the machine budget is
   silent for that long. It may be the time to the first wave.
3. **A repeating one-shot whose last play ends in the same frame as its next firing is dropped for a
   whole interval**: the budget marks the old voice finished and removes the new submission with it.
   Rare (the clip has to end on the frame the interval comes round).
